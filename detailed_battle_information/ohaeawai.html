Battle of Ōhaeawai

In the next battle involving British troops, at Ōhaeawai, soldiers attempted to storm Kawiti’s pā but came under intense fire from Māori, who shot from hidden positions. The soldiers were forced to retreat, and a hundred men were killed or wounded. FitzRoy didn’t get the chance to make amends after the setbacks at Kororāreka and Ōhaeawai. He lost his post shortly afterwards.
