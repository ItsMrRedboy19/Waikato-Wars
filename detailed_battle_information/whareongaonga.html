Te Kooti Lands in Whareongaonga

Te Kooti and his Ringatū followers arrived at Whareongaonga on 10 July after escaping imprisonment. Their presence alarmed the Government, already dealing with hostilities led by Tītokowaru in South Taranaki. Te Kooti refused to surrender, citing mistrust after being held without trial, and aimed to establish a rival kingship to Tāwhiao. Biggs assembled forces to confront Te Kooti, including militia, Mounted Rifles, and kūpapa. Additional parties from Wairoa and Napier joined the pursuit, led by Colonel Whitmore. Te Kooti's group departed Whareongaonga on 15 July, narrowly avoiding interception, and moved inland to Paparatu. Biggs attempted to cut them off but was delayed. On 20 July, government forces engaged Te Kooti's followers near Paparatu. Te Kooti's strategic attacks forced a retreat, allowing him to seize supplies, arms, and horses. This marked the beginning of a series of confrontations.

Te Kooti's War

Date: July 1868 - May 1872

Location: East Coast and central North Island, New Zealand

Belligerents

United Kingdom: Colony of New Zealand, Ngāti Porou, Ngāti Kahungunu

Te Kooti: Ringatū adherents, Pai Mārire adherents, Ngāi Tūhoe, Ngāti Hineuru, Rongowhakaata
